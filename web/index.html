Race → Training Paces

Daniels & Gilbert equations, solved in your browser

Enter one recent, all-out race (or a VDOT) to get Daniels' E/M/T/I/R training paces per mile, per km and as treadmill mph, plus equivalent race times at ten distances. Everything runs on this page; nothing you type is sent or stored anywhere.
